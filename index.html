Founding Partner · Senior Counsel

Fredrick Muiruri, SC

Managing Partner · Advocates of the High Court of Kenya

Called to the bar in 1999, Fredrick Muiruri SC has built an unparalleled reputation across constitutional law, corporate litigation, and commercial disputes. A graduate of the University of Nairobi School of Law and holder of an LLM from SOAS University of London, he has argued landmark cases before the Supreme Court of Kenya, the Court of Appeal, and international arbitral tribunals.

His practice spans corporate governance, constitutional petitions, and complex commercial arbitration. He is a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators and serves on the LSK Professional Committee.
